Institutional fluency

Strong communication without flattening the institution.

Higher education communication has to navigate expertise, governance, student experience, public purpose, accessibility, trust, and limited attention at the same time. The work needs more than polished language; it needs a communicator who can understand the system around the message.

My experience spans university engaged media, student journalism, civic learning, teaching, research communication, magazine leadership, audio production, legal analysis, and emerging technology. I can help shape a single public-facing project or improve the editorial structure that supports a program over time.

Working process

Start with context, then build the communication

  1. Listen

    Understand the institutional setting, stakeholders, internal constraints, history, and what success would mean for the people doing the work.

  2. Frame

    Define the audience, purpose, editorial angle, evidence, voice, and decisions the communication needs to support.

  3. Build

    Create the content, workflow, publication structure, facilitation plan, or advising system that fits the actual campus environment.

  4. Learn

    Review what reached people, where the process held, and what should become a stronger institutional habit next time.
