Working process

From polished narrative to useful clarity

  1. Surface

    Identify the visible message, the private concern, the audience reality, and the decision the communication is trying to avoid or support.

  2. Interrogate

    Ask what has stopped being questioned, who has not been heard, and where the stated values diverge from the lived workflow.

  3. Translate

    Turn complexity into plain language that preserves nuance, evidence, and stakes without asking readers to decode internal jargon.

  4. Build

    Create the memo, message map, playbook, editorial workflow, talking points, or prototype that helps the team move.

  5. Transfer

    Leave behind usable documents, decision rules, editorial standards, and a clearer path for the next communication problem.
